How this works

Pick a book, then a commentary on it. The whole commentary loads into this pane so you can scroll and read it before committing to anything.

The bar above tracks coverage against Sefaria's own segment count for that work. It reads COMPLETE only when every section came back with text; otherwise it says PARTIAL and lists what is missing. Download stays available either way — you just always know which one you are getting.

Downloads land in your browser's Downloads folder, or next to this app in sefaria_downloads\ via Save to folder.

Download anything in the library

This tab is not limited to commentaries. Every text Sefaria carries is in the tree on the left — Tanakh, Mishnah, Talmud, Midrash, Halakhah, Kabbalah, Chasidut, Liturgy, Jewish Thought, Responsa, and the commentaries too.

Click a text to see its structure and the versions available for it, then Download now for a single work, or Add to queue to line up as many as you like and fetch them one after another.

The + beside any title queues it without leaving the tree, and Queue everything here takes every text in the section you are browsing, sub-sections included.

Format and destination are set once, in the queue column, and apply to Download now as well: with to folder ticked everything lands next to this app in sefaria_downloads\, and unticked it goes to your browser's Downloads folder.
