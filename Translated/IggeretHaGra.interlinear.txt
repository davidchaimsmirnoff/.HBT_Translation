All of Israel (N"E: and the desire of the Name - may He be blessed, all the upper and lower ones desire it), and I travel in peace, blessed be the Lord. And also.

You know that I have placed my children, my heart beating for them, and all my precious books, and I was like a stranger in another land, and I have placed everything, etc.

וִידוּעַ, כִּי כׇּל הָעוֹלָם-הַזֶּה הַכֹּל הֶבֶל, וְכׇל הַשַּׁעֲשׁוּעִים הַכֹּל כְּלָא, וְאוֹי לַהָרוֹדְפִים אַחַר הַהֶבֶל אֵין בּוֹ מוֹעִיל.
And it is known that all of this world is vanity, and all pleasures are vanity, and woe to those who chase after vanity, for there is no benefit in it.

וְאַל תִּקְנֵא בְּעֹשֶׁר, כִּי "יֵשׁ וְגוֹ' עֹשֶׁר שָׁמוּר לִבְעָלָיו לְרַעָתוֹ", "כַּאֲשֶׁר יָצָא מִבֶּטֶן אִמּוֹ עָרוֹם יָשׁוּב"

A man dies and half his desire is in his hand (Kohelet Rabbah, 1). 'What advantage does a man have in all his toil?' etc. (Kohelet 1:3). Remember.

The earlier ones who were before us, whose entire love and desire and joy have already been lost (there) and they receive many judgments for this.

וּמָה-הֲנָאָה לָאָדָם שֶׁסּוֹפוֹ לִפְרֹשׁ לְעָפָר רִמָּה וְתוֹלַעַת, וְכׇל הֲנָאוֹת יִתְהַפְּכוּ לוֹ בַּקֶּבֶר לְמָרָה וְהַמָּוֶת כָּרוּךְ בָּאָדָם.
And what benefit is there for a man whose end is to be laid in the dust, in worms and maggots? And all his pleasures will be turned in his grave into bitterness, and death is bound up with man.

וּמָה הָעוֹלָם-הַזֶּה, כִּי כׇּל יָמָיו כַּעַס וּמַכְאֻבִּים, גַּם בַּלַּיְלָה אֵינֶנּוּ מַנִּיחַ לוֹ לִישֹׁן, וְהַמָּוֶת אֵינוֹ מְקַוֶּה,
